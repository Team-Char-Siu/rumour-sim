# Sources

## Implementation starting point

1. Le Nguyen. Simulation 102: Agent-Based Disease Modeling. Medium, 2023.
   https://medium.com/@ln8378/simulation-102-agent-based-disease-modeling-17307f2de2f3

The project adapts a mobile-agent contagion approach. Friendship, loyalty,
correction openness, sharing decisions, subject awareness and avoidance are
project extensions, not a claim of faithful article reproduction. Retain
ARTICLE markers where present; they identify attributed adaptations, not exact copies.
